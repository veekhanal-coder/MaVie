# 🌸 MiVee — For Burmese Mums

> A community-first e-commerce platform connecting mothers in Yangon — shop, connect, and grow together.

---

## 📱 What is MiVee?

MiVee is a mobile-first app designed specifically for mothers in Yangon, Myanmar. It combines a curated baby and maternal products shop with a verified community space — so mums can shop trusted brands, join local playgroups, and connect with other mothers, all in one place.

---

## ✨ Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 🛍️ **Shop** | Browse and buy baby & maternal products from trusted brands (Cetaphil, Pigeon, and more) |
| 👩‍👩‍👧 **Community** | A verified-mothers-only space to share, ask, and support each other |
| 🧸 **Playgroups** | Discover and sign up for local playgroups across Yangon |
| 🍪 **Snackies** | Bite-sized tips and content for mums |
| 🔔 **Alerts** | Order updates and community notifications |

---

## 🗺️ Delivery Coverage

Delivery is available across all **19 townships in Yangon**.

---

## 💳 Payment Methods

- KBZPay
- TrueMoney
- Wave Money
- AYAPay
- Cash on Delivery (COD)

> 💡 No credit card required — all payments via local Myanmar mobile wallets.

## 🔒 Privacy & Safety

- Community access requires **mother verification**
- Children's data is handled in compliance with **Myanmar PDPA guidelines**
- Privacy-first design throughout

## 🗂️ Prototype

This repository contains the **MiVee Prototype ** — a fully interactive HTML prototype covering 16 screens:

- Home, Products, Cart
- Address Selection (all 19 Yangon townships)
- Payment & Order Confirmation with tracking
- Playgroups, Snackies, Search, Notifications
- Community Join Flow & Approved Community View
- Membership Tiers, Login / Sign Up, Profile & Settings

### Running the Prototype Locally

```bash
# No installation needed — just open in your browser
open index.html
```

Or simply double-click `index.html` on your desktop.

## 📦 Product Sourcing

MiVee sources products through **regional wholesale distributors** of international baby and maternal brands present in Myanmar retail (Watsons, Boots, H&M). This is a reseller model — not a direct retail partnership.
